Current data posture

What the site currently does.

01
Website analytics

The site currently loads PostHog on EU-hosted endpoints for page-view, page-leave and selected interaction events. Session recording, heatmaps, surveys, dead-click capture and console-log capture are disabled in the current configuration.

02
Brief request form

The contact form can collect the email address, name, company, website, business question, known competitors and timing supplied by the visitor. Submissions are sent to a RivalFrame Supabase function for intake processing.

03
Email fallback

Visitors may also contact RivalFrame at rivalframeintel@gmail.com. Final privacy information must accurately cover the email provider and actual retention workflow used for customer correspondence.

04
No payment checkout yet

The public site currently presents pilot pricing but does not yet include a configured payment checkout. Payment-processor terms and privacy information must be added only after the actual provider is selected.

05
Research limitations

RivalFrame provides market research and recommendations based primarily on public information. Public information can change after delivery. RivalFrame does not guarantee commercial outcomes, sales conversion, market performance, AI-search rankings, citations or mentions.
