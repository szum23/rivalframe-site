The RivalFrame method

We turn market movement into a business decision.

Scroll through the four layers of a RivalFrame brief.

01 / SIGNAL

What changed?

We identify meaningful competitive movement across pricing, positioning, launches, product changes, reviews, buyer language and emerging substitutes.

02 / EVIDENCE

Is it real or noise?

Material findings stay connected to public sources. Repeated patterns, freshness, contradictions and uncertainty are made explicit instead of hidden.
