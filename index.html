Signal → Evidence → Implication → Decision

One question. Four layers of clarity.

The same market signal is resolved into evidence, interpreted against your context, and turned into a recommendation. The goal is not more information. It is less uncertainty.

01 / SIGNAL

Start wider than the obvious competitors.

Pricing, positioning, launches, reviews, hiring, buyer language and substitutes all enter the frame before most of them are discarded.

02 / EVIDENCE

A plausible claim is not enough.

Every material finding is checked for source, date and corroboration. Fact and interpretation stay visibly separate.

03 / IMPLICATION

A change is not automatically a threat.

We work out which buyers, deals or segments it actually affects — and whether it changes your position at all.

04 / DECISION

You hired us for a recommendation.

The brief ends with what we would do, what we would not do yet, the trade-off, and what would make us change our mind.
