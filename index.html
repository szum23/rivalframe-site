How it works

One question in. A clearer decision out.

01 / SCOPE

You tell us the decision.

Company, website, question, known competitors and any deadline. Five lines can be enough.

02 / RESEARCH

We map the evidence.

First-party pages, pricing, product changes, public reviews, market discussions and other relevant sources.

03 / VERIFY

We separate signal from noise.

Important claims are checked, sources are preserved and weak evidence is labelled instead of dressed up as certainty.

04 / DECIDE

You receive the implications.

A concise brief with findings, risks, recommended actions and what deserves testing next.
